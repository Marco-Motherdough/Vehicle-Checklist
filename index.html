Driver, Vehicle & Communication Policy

Motherdough Pty Ltd  ·  Compliance is mandatory for all authorised drivers.

1. Purpose ▾

This policy outlines the responsibilities, standards, and expectations for all employees authorised to operate company vehicles on behalf of Motherdough.

  • Protect company assets
  • Ensure safe vehicle operation
  • Ensure proper communication
  • Ensure accountability and handovers
  • Promote efficient use of company resources
  • Minimise unnecessary costs and operational disruptions
Compliance with this policy is mandatory.
2. Company Vehicle Use ▾

Company vehicles are provided strictly for business purposes. Employees may not:

  • Use company vehicles for personal purposes or errands
  • Allow unauthorised passengers in company vehicles
  • Allow unauthorised persons to drive company vehicles
  • Use company vehicles outside approved business activities

Drivers must hold a valid South African driver's licence at all times. Any suspension, expiry, or restriction must be reported immediately.

3. Vehicle Storage ▾

Company vehicles may not be parked overnight at an employee's residence.

Vehicles must be returned to the approved Motherdough location at the end of each working day unless prior written permission has been granted by management.

Failure to comply will result in disciplinary action.
4. Daily Vehicle Inspection ▾

The assigned driver is responsible for conducting a daily inspection before commencing duties. The inspection includes:

  • Fuel level
  • Oil level
  • Water / Coolant level
  • Tyres
  • Lights
  • Windscreen
  • Wipers
  • Vehicle cleanliness
  • Visible damage
  • Dashboard warning lights
The Daily Vehicle Inspection Form must be completed daily. Any defects must be reported immediately.
5. Vehicle Care & Maintenance ▾

Drivers are expected to:

  • Keep vehicles clean and presentable inside and outside
  • Monitor oil and water levels
  • Report warning lights, unusual sounds, and tyre damage immediately
  • Report accidents immediately
  • Lock and secure the vehicle whenever unattended
Ignoring or failing to report vehicle issues may result in disciplinary action.
6. Fuel Management ▾

Fuel may only be purchased using approved company procedures. Whenever fuel is purchased:

  • A clear photo of the fuel receipt must be posted immediately to the Driver Handover WhatsApp Group
  • Receipts may not be submitted at the end of the day
  • Photos must be clear and readable

The receipt must clearly show: date, time, petrol station, amount, and litres purchased.

Failure to submit receipts immediately may result in disciplinary action.
7 & 8. Company Loyalty Cards ▾

Motherdough provides a BP Rewards Card and a Pick n Pay Smart Shopper Card (7353 2804 0377 1419). These remain company property and must:

  • Remain inside the vehicle at all times
  • Never be removed from the vehicle
  • Be used whenever purchases are made at BP or Pick n Pay

The rewards, points, and cashback belong to Motherdough. Employees may not use personal loyalty cards for company purchases.

Using personal loyalty cards for company purchases is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.
9–11. Communication Standards ▾

Communication forms part of every employee's responsibilities. Sending a message does not automatically mean the matter has been communicated successfully. The responsibility remains with the employee until the matter has been acknowledged, understood, attended to, and resolved.

"I sent a message" is not considered completion of communication.

On WhatsApp, employees must acknowledge instructions. Acceptable responses include: Received, Understood, Will do, Done, Completed.

Phone calls should only be made after a message has been sent and reasonable time has been allowed for a response. Group calls are not to be used to contact specific individuals.

12. Purchasing & Shopping ▾

When shopping for company supplies:

  • Calculate the estimated total while shopping
  • Obtain approval before reaching the checkout
  • Do not wait until standing at the till before requesting approval
  • Submit receipts immediately after purchase
13 & 14. Daily Handover & Outstanding Tasks ▾

A handover is mandatory every day — whether the employee is working the following day or not. The following must be communicated before clocking out:

  • Outstanding deliveries and collections
  • Vehicle issues and fuel requirements
  • Maintenance concerns
  • Stock transfer issues
  • Tasks not completed
  • Location of keys and company bank card

Any task not completed must be communicated on the designated WhatsApp group before clocking out. The next shift must know what was completed, what remains outstanding, and what action is required.

15. Clocking In & Out ▾

Employees must clock in and clock out from the bakery or approved workplace:

61 Cabriere Street, Franschhoek, 6970

Employees may not:

  • Clock out while driving
  • Clock out from company vehicles
  • Clock out after arriving home

Employees clock out when work is completed, not when they arrive home.

16. Accidents, Fines & Violations ▾

All accidents must be reported immediately. Drivers are personally responsible for:

  • Traffic, speeding, and parking fines
  • Traffic violations
Vehicle damage resulting from negligence may result in financial liability as permitted by law.
17. Prohibited Conduct ▾

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
  • Smoking or vaping in company vehicles
  • Using a cell phone while driving without hands-free equipment (provided once off by the company — replacement is the driver's responsibility)
  • Allowing unauthorised drivers
  • Using company vehicles for personal use
  • Using personal loyalty cards during company purchases
  • Removing company loyalty cards from vehicles
18. Failure to Comply ▾

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

  • Verbal warning
  • Written warning
  • Final written warning
  • Disciplinary hearing
  • Termination of employment where appropriate
